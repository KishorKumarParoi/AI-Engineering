Purpose
Define a reusable AI appointment-booking agent that understands customer requests, checks live availability, books,
reschedules or cancels appointments, and confirms outcomes across messaging and voice. It should support spas, salons,
clinics, doctors’ offices and other appointment-based businesses through configurable business profiles—not separate
custom agents for every industry.

Core principle: One shared booking orchestration layer and business configuration model, with channel-specific
experiences for text and voice.

1. Product Overview
The agent acts as a virtual receptionist. It identifies intent, understands the requested service, collects required details,
checks the business’s actual availability and completes the action only after the customer confirms.

1.1 Goals
•

Enable 24/7 appointment booking without requiring staff intervention.

•

Provide consistent booking capability across social messaging, website chat and phone.

•

Reduce missed inquiries, manual scheduling effort and booking errors.

•

Support multiple locations, services, staff, durations, business hours and booking policies.

•

Allow business configuration without custom development for each new tenant.

1.2 Suggested initial exclusions
•

Clinical diagnosis, treatment recommendations or medical advice beyond approved safety scripts.

•

Full CRM replacement, payroll, inventory management or marketing automation.

•

Complex recurring/group bookings unless explicitly added to scope.

•

Payments/deposits unless payment integration and compliance requirements are approved.

4. Channel-Specific Requirements
4.1 Messaging: Facebook Messenger, Instagram Messenger and website chat
•

Support inbound/outbound text and channel-appropriate formatting; show typing/processing indicators where supported.

•

Use quick replies, buttons or structured choices where available, with text fallback.

•

Handle multi-part messages, typos, corrections, short replies and natural-language date/time expressions.

•

Website widget supports configurable branding, greeting, launcher and responsive layout.

•

Respect Meta platform permissions, messaging windows, opt-in rules and approved outbound templates.

•

Provide a concise text equivalent if a channel cannot render a requested format.

4.2 Phone voice agent
•

Answer inbound calls with configured greeting; identify business/location from number or routing metadata.

•

Use speech recognition, dialogue orchestration and text-to-speech with barge-in and natural turn-taking.

•

Speak clearly, avoid long lists, repeat important details and confirm critical fields when confidence is low.

•

Handle silence, noise, interruptions, DTMF where available, voicemail and dropped calls.

•

Offer live transfer or callback capture when staff are unavailable.

•

Provide automated-agent and recording/consent disclosures where required.

•

Never invent availability or claim a booking is complete before scheduler confirmation.

4.3 Channel parity
All channels use the same booking rules and source-of-truth scheduling APIs. Presentation may differ, but eligibility,
availability, confirmation, cancellation and audit outcomes must remain consistent.

7. Data, Privacy & Security
•

Tenant isolation across conversations, customer records, calendars and credentials.

•

Collect only data necessary for the request; define retention and deletion policies.

•

Encrypt data in transit and at rest; role-based access and audit logs.

•

Protect against prompt injection, malicious links, data exfiltration and unauthorized tool/API calls.

•

For healthcare, assess applicable legal/regulatory/contractual obligations; minimize sensitive health data and do not
diagnose or prescribe.

•

Support consent/disclosure requirements for calls, recordings, messaging and data use.

•

Redact logs where configured; do not store unnecessary sensitive or payment-card data.

9. Reference Conversation & Booking Flow
1

Welcome: greet customer and identify business using channel context.

2

Intent: determine booking, reschedule, cancellation or question.

3

Service: identify requested service and clarify ambiguity.

4

Location/provider: collect preferences if applicable.

5

Time: ask for date/time or range; clarify ambiguous expressions.

6

Availability: query live scheduler and offer valid slots.

7

Customer details: collect and validate required information.

8

Review: summarize appointment and policies; obtain explicit confirmation.

9

Commit: create appointment and verify success response.

10

Confirm: provide reference, date/time, location, service and next steps.

Required exception paths
•

No availability: ask for alternate date/time/provider/location.

•

Slot conflict: re-query and offer alternatives.

•

Customer correction: update state and re-check affected fields.

•

Unclear speech/text: ask one concise clarification; do not guess critical details.

•

Integration unavailable: explain limitation and offer handoff/callback; do not represent an unconfirmed request as booked.

•

Medical/urgent request: follow approved safety routing; no diagnosis or treatment advice.

10. Acceptance Criteria / Definition of Done
•

New booking completes end-to-end on all four channels.

•

Agent checks live availability and commits to configured source-of-truth scheduler.

•

Explicit confirmation required before create, reschedule or cancel.

•

Retries and duplicate events do not create duplicate appointments.

•

Slot conflict triggers fresh availability check and alternatives.

•

Reschedule/cancel verifies booking and applies configured policies.

•

Supported business configuration changes do not require code deployment.

•

Human handoff includes summary, collected details and escalation reason.

•

Analytics/logs capture outcomes while honoring privacy and retention settings.

•

Tests cover happy path, ambiguity, no availability, API failure, interruption and recovery.
