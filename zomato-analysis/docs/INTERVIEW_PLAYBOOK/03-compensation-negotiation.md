## 2. Anatomy of the Offer: What to Negotiate

1. **Fixed Base**: The bedrock of your comp. Do not trade base for unvested paper equity unless the company is pre-IPO with immediate liquidity rounds.
2. **Joining / Retention Bonus**: Always ask for a two-year distributed signing bonus (e.g., ₹20L Year 1 + ₹15L Year 2) to bridge any unvested stock left behind at your previous employer.
3. **RSU Vesting Schedule**:
   - Push for **quarterly vesting** (25% per year) rather than annual cliffs.
   - For high-growth public companies, negotiate back-loaded or front-loaded refreshers (e.g., 30/30/20/20).
4. **Relocation & Tax Benefits**: Executive relocation allowance, car lease program, and Section 80 tax optimizations.

## 3. High-Leverage Negotiation Scripts & Tactics

### Script 1: Anchoring using Bengaluru / Tier-1 Market Bands
> *"Thank you for the initial offer. I'm very excited about the scale and mission of leading the AI Systems and Lakehouse architecture here. Based on my technical ownership across 20k QPS real-time distributed platforms and the current Principal Architect market band in Bengaluru for Tier-1 product tech firms, the competitive total compensation target is ₹1.65 Cr, with a base of ₹85L. Given the critical scope of this role, how close can we get to this band?"*

### Script 2: Leveraging Competing Offers Without Burning Bridges
> *"I have received a competing offer from a Series-E platform firm with a Year 1 Total Comp of ₹1.72 Cr. However, having met the team here, your technical challenges in multimodal dispute RAG and real-time Kafka architecture align much more closely with my architectural vision. If we can adjust the equity grant to ₹60L per annum and match the base at ₹82L, I am ready to sign today and decline the other discussions."*

### Script 3: Countering "City Band Reductions" (e.g., Pune / Hyderabad vs. BLR)
> *"I understand the company has regional location tiers, but as a Principal Systems Architect, the architectural decisions, SLOs, and multi-region failover strategies I deliver govern the entire national infrastructure, not a local branch office. My impact is company-wide, and my compensation should reflect the nationwide value generated rather than local real estate indices."*

## 4. Key Rules of Engagement
- **Never reveal current CTC upfront**: Anchor to market expectations and scope of responsibility.
- **Get everything in writing**: Verbal promises of equity refreshers or future promotions mean nothing without addendum signatures.
- **Evaluate ESOP Liquidity**: Ask for 409A valuation, liquidation preferences, and last secondary sale terms before valuing private company equity.
